m3ustreamz

Curated M3U playlists of free, legal streams, built from iptv-org. Find a playlist or a channel, then open it in Mediagg.

    Opening a playlist in Mediagg

    1. Tap Open in Mediagg. With the app installed it opens there; without it, the playlist opens in Mediagg in your browser.
    2. Choose where it goes. Subscriptions keeps it up to date as we rebuild it; Playlists takes the channels in it today.
    3. Prefer another player? The copy icon beside the file name copies its address for any app that reads M3U.
    Rebuilt with python3 scripts/build.py from the iptv-org API. github.com/c4Entertainment/m3ustreamz
